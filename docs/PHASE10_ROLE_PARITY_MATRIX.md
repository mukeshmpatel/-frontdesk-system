# Wyndham Garden Salina Digital Employee Role Parity Matrix

Source basis: existing AAIQ property workflows, Sample Environment roles, Front Desk/Housekeeping/Maintenance operations, Forks & Fingers inventory controls, workforce rules, reporting facts, and the approved Phase 10 standard. Role families are mapped into the existing six governed Digital Employees; this does not create new agents.

| Property role | Physical-only (stays human) | Digital-capable (must remain with Digital Employee) |
|---|---|---|
| Front Desk Agent | Hand keycard to guest; physically inspect ID/card; physically escort guest | Check-in/out data; reservation/rate/room assignment; approved upsell; guest answers; governed recovery; folio and shift close |
| Reservations | None ordinarily | Direct/OTA bookings; changes/cancellations; group blocks; rooming lists; pre-arrival readiness and upsell |
| Guest Services / Concierge | Deliver items; physically escort or assist guest | Property/local information; service dispatch; accessibility/pet workflow; lost-and-found matching and communication |
| Housekeeping Supervisor | Clean or physically inspect room | Room board; assignments; status/evidence review; rework; supply forecast; productivity and written coaching |
| Maintenance / Engineering | Repair, lift, replace, meter or physically inspect equipment | Triage; diagnosis; dispatch; parts/vendor coordination; evidence verification; preventive-maintenance reporting |
| Restaurant & Bar Manager — Forks & Fingers | Prepare/serve food and drink; count or receive stock physically | Opening/closing reconciliation; cover forecast; staffing plan; menu/yield controls; inventory reconciliation; food-safety log review |
| Sales & Revenue Manager | Physical tours and in-person events | Rates; demand analysis; proposals; account follow-up; group forecast; displacement and variance analysis |
| Purchasing / Inventory | Count, receive, store or move goods physically | Replenishment; vendor comparison; PO preparation; receipt/invoice matching; duplicate, price, yield and usage controls |
| Accounting / Bookkeeping | Physical cash custody where required | Coding; bank/card/PMS/POS reconciliation; AP preparation; P&L/cash/budget variance; vendor correspondence |
| Night Auditor | Physical cash/key/security tasks | Pre-audit; revenue/tax/payment/ledger reconciliation; idempotent EOD; business-date roll; GM morning packet |
| HR / People | In-person interviews or legally reserved employment decisions | Scheduling; time exceptions; recruiting administration; onboarding; training/access records; objective coaching evidence |
| Security / Loss Prevention | Patrol; intervene; emergency response; physical evidence custody | Incident triage; access/evidence timeline; investigation support; access review; holds and retention controls |
| Marketing / Reputation | Physical content capture where necessary | Review investigation/response; consented campaigns; brand QA; website/listing audit and approved update |
| General Manager oversight | Physical emergency command and legally reserved executive decisions | Department roll-up; financial control; guest/brand risk resolution; coverage/productivity/compliance oversight |
| Banquet / Conference Operations | Room setup, service and physical event execution | BEO timeline; staffing/setup/inventory plan; milestone/change control; settlement and profitability recap |

Human handoffs are valid only when categorized `PHYSICAL` or `LEGALLY_RESERVED`. Provider unavailability creates a manual-fallback record and does not transfer the Digital Employee's digital responsibility to a person.
